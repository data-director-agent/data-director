# Notifications

Nobody should have to watch a queue to find out that something needs them.

**Your team is emailed** at the address your deployment's profile names for it: when a request is
routed to your team, when one nobody has taken is sent to you again, and when the researcher
replies, withdraws a request or re-opens it (re-opening is how a researcher disagrees with how you
resolved it). Your own moves, and your colleagues', send nothing.

**The address is the team's, not yours.** Data Director keeps no one's email address, so it emails
the team's shared mailbox. It can also be your helpdesk's intake address, so that each message
becomes a ticket there. The record in Data Director stays the one that counts: answer in the app.

**A message holds a subject and a link, never research content.** Follow the link and sign in to
read the matter.

**Your team chooses** which of these it is emailed about. Whoever runs your deployment sets the
address and the choice in the profile (`notify`, `notify_on`); the Settings page, under
Notifications, shows what is set. A team with no address is not emailed: look at **Requests**.

**A request nobody takes is sent again.** Where the profile sets how long a request may wait
(`reroute_after`), one that nobody has taken in that time is sent to your team again, and the
researcher is told. It is sent again each time that period passes, until someone takes it.

**The researcher is told in the app.** When you ask them something, write to them, resolve a request
or hand it back, **Updates** in their banner says so.
