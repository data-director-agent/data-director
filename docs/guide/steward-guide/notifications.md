# Notifications

Nobody should have to watch a queue to find out that something needs them.

!!! note "Not built yet"
    Notifications are [#378](https://github.com/data-director-agent/data-director/issues/378). Email comes first.

**You are told** when an escalation is routed to your team. Once you take it, you are told when the
researcher replies, withdraws it, re-opens it, or disagrees with how you resolved it.

**The researcher is told** when you reply, hand an escalation back or resolve it.

**A notification holds a link and a short subject, never research content.** Follow the link and
sign in to read the matter.

**You choose** which of these you are told about.

An instance may send notifications to its own helpdesk instead. The record in Data Director then
stays the one that counts. Whether and how this works is still to be decided, under
[#378](https://github.com/data-director-agent/data-director/issues/378).
