than policy allows.

## Your team's projects

Only where the profile sets `visibility: scope`, this lists the projects whose owners chose your
team as their support team. It shows each project's phase, its recent outcomes, and anything waiting
for a review. Listing a project counts as looking at it, and is recorded and shown to the
researcher. Under the default `visibility: escalated`, this list holds only the projects in your
queue.

## The community overview

Counts across your team's scope (Blueprint §4.1, "an overview of data practices within a research
community"):

- the repositories and metadata standards researchers chose;
- referrals and abstentions, by reason;
- escalations: how many, how old, and how long they took to resolve.

It names no project or person. Small counts are suppressed, so that no one can be singled out.
Viewing it records nothing on any project, because it reads none.

## Compliance signals

How many projects are stopped at each mandatory review ([Referrals and reviews](referrals-and-reviews.md)).
These show where researchers get stuck, and where guidance or policy may need to change (§4.1,
"monitor compliance and recommend remediation").
