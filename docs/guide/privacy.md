# Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal data is processed when you visit this documentation website,
<https://data-director-agent.github.io/data-director/>, and when you take part in the project
through GitHub. It follows the information the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) says a
privacy notice must contain under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## In short

- The site has **no analytics, advertising or tracking** of its own, and sets **no cookies**.
- The site is hosted by **GitHub Pages**. GitHub, not the project, logs visitors' IP addresses.
- Pages load **fonts from Google**, and a few pages load **badge images** from other services.
  Those services can see your IP address.
- If you **raise an issue or contribute code** on GitHub, what you write, and your GitHub
  username, are public.
- This notice is about the website and project. It is **not** about the Data Director app, which
  an institution runs for itself (see [Software you run](#software-you-run)).

We do not make decisions about you by solely automated means, and we do not profile visitors.

## Cookies and similar technologies

The project sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising. The site's search and
navigation work in your browser, and the theme may keep small settings, such as search state, in
your browser's local or session storage. That storage stays on your device and is not sent to
us. It is strictly necessary for features you ask for, so it does not need your consent under
the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR). Clear it any time in your browser
settings.

GitHub, Google and the other services below may use their own technical logs. Their notices
are linked from this page.

## Hosting on GitHub Pages

The site is published with [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages). When you visit,
GitHub receives your IP address and browser details, and may log them for security and
operational purposes. GitHub is responsible for that data under its
[General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
It is not shared with us, and we cannot see who has visited.

You can stop the fonts and images loading by blocking requests to those domains with your
browser or an extension. The site then falls back to your system fonts and shows the images'
alternative text.

## Sharing your data

We do not sell personal data. Personal data goes to:

- **GitHub, Inc.** (and its group companies), which hosts the site, the code, issues and
  discussions, and acts as a processor or controller as its terms describe.
- **Google, Codecov, Shields.io, Zenodo and the University of Sheffield media service**, as
  set out above, only to the extent your browser contacts them.
- **The public.** Issues, comments, commits and sign-offs on GitHub are visible to anyone,
  and may be copied by others, such as forks, mirrors, search engines and web archives.

## Transfers outside the UK

GitHub, Google, Codecov and several of the other services above are based in or use
infrastructure in the United States and elsewhere. Where they transfer personal data outside the
UK, they rely on safeguards such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (the
"UK–US data bridge"), where the recipient is certified under it, or the ICO's International Data
Transfer Agreement or Addendum. Each service's own notice gives the detail. We do not
transfer personal data to anyone ourselves.

## Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to:

- **be informed** about how your data is used, as this notice does;
- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- have inaccurate data **rectified**;
- have your data **erased**, in some circumstances;
- **restrict** how we use it, in some circumstances;
- **data portability**, where we process data you gave us by consent or contract, by automated
  means (this is unlikely to apply here); and
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interests.

**You have the right to object to our use of your data on the basis of legitimate interests.**
Write to us at **[contact address]** and say why. We will stop unless we can show compelling
grounds that override your interests, or the processing is needed for legal claims.

We rely on consent for nothing, so there is none to withdraw. If that changes, we will ask you
first and say how to withdraw it.

To use a right, write to **[contact address]**. We will reply within one month. We may need to
confirm who you are first. We cannot act on data held by GitHub, Google or the other services;
write to them.

## Software you run

This notice covers the website and the project's work on GitHub. The Data Director **app** and
agents run on a computer or server that you or your institution operates. When an institution
runs them, it decides why and how personal data is processed, and must give its own privacy
information to its users ([what an institution's notice should cover](operations/privacy.md)).
[What is recorded](user-guide/records.md) describes the records the app keeps, and the
[operations guide](operations/index.md) covers the AI models an instance may call. The project does not receive that data.
