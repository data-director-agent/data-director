# Working on a project with others

Several researchers can work on one project together. They share its inventory, documents,
decisions and conversations, and each can carry the work forward.

## Adding someone

You add people from the project's **Members** tab if you are an owner of the project.

1. Open the project and choose the **Members** tab.
2. Under **Add a member**, type their ORCID iD, such as `0000-0002-1825-0097`. They need not have
   signed in to Data Director yet.
3. Choose their role, then select **Add to the project**.

Data Director never asks for or keeps an email address for a member. It keeps only their ORCID
iD. The Members tab names each member by their iD, and by their name too once they have kept
facts from their own ORCID record in the project ([Using your ORCID record](orcid-facts.md)).

## Roles

A project has two roles:

- A **member** reads the project, takes part in its conversations, adds an inventory or
  documentation, edits documents as new proposed versions, and keeps their own ORCID facts.
- An **owner** can do all of that, and also approves or rejects document versions, decides the
  facts only an owner may, adds and removes members, changes their roles, and deletes the
  project.

Whoever creates a project is its first owner. A project always has at least one owner, so the last
owner cannot leave or be made a member until someone else is an owner.

The tab offers you only what your role allows. Data Director checks every change again, whatever
the page offered.

## What members see of one another's work

Everyone on the project sees all of it:

- every conversation, including one another's messages, each headed by the name of the person who
  sent it ("You" for your own);
- the inventory and documentation anyone attached;
- every document version and every decision, with its reason;
- the facts anyone kept from their own ORCID record.

The agents are never told your name or your iD from your messages. They see only the facts a
member chose to keep from their ORCID record ([Using your ORCID record](orcid-facts.md)).

## Leaving, and removing someone

To leave a project, select **Leave this project** on the Members tab and confirm. An owner can
remove a member with **Remove** next to their name. Either way, the person no longer sees the
project from that moment. Everything they did in it keeps their name, and an owner can add them
again later.

## If someone else sent a message first

When two members write in the same conversation at once, the second message is not sent. Data
Director shows the conversation as it now stands and keeps your message in the box, so that you
can read what changed and send it again.
