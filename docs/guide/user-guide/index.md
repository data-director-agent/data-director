# User guide

This guide is for you if you are a researcher with data to look after. It starts with the
essentials and then covers each part of the app in turn. You do not need to read it in order, and
you do not need to read all of it: the first two pages are enough to begin.

## Start here

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md): open the app, start a project and have your first
   conversation.
2. [The workflow](workflow.md): the stages of looking after research data, from planning to after
   publication, and how to join at the right one.
3. [The agents](agents.md): the specialists the Data Director asks for you, what each helps with
   and what each will not decide for you.

## Doing the work

4. [Using the conversation](conversation.md): the home page, writing to the Data Director,
   attaching files and reading a reply.
5. [Making an inventory](making-an-inventory.md): describe your dataset's files without sharing
   them.
6. [Attaching a document](attaching-a-document.md): give the Data Director a proposal, award
   letter or policy to read.
7. [Deciding project facts](project-facts.md): confirm, change or reject what the agents propose.
8. [Running an agent](running-an-agent.md): open a specialist directly rather than through the
   conversation.
9. [Depositing your data](depositing.md): the package the Data Director builds, and how you take
   it to your repository.

## Understanding what you get

10. [Understanding outcomes](outcomes.md): what it means when a reply succeeds, abstains, is
    referred or fails.
11. [What the Data Director records](records.md): what is kept about each run and conversation,
    where you can see it and who else can.

## Working with other people

12. [Working on a project with others](members.md): add colleagues by ORCID iD, their roles and
    what members see of one another's work.
13. [Using your ORCID record](orcid-facts.md): bring facts from your public ORCID record into a
    project.
14. [Asking a data steward](ask-a-data-steward.md): hand a matter to a person at your institution
    and follow the request.

## About this alpha

15. [Alpha usage agreement](usage-agreement.md): use test data only, and treat advice as a draft.
16. [Giving feedback](feedback.md): tell the project where its advice or its specifications fall
    short, without writing code.
