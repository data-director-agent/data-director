# Using facts from your ORCID record

Agents drafting your metadata record ask for things you have often recorded already: your
name, your employer, your research keywords, your grants. If you sign in with ORCID, you can
choose facts from your **public** ORCID record and keep them in a project, so that its agents can
fill these in for you.

## Choosing what to keep

1. Open the project's page and choose **Researchers** in the list.
2. Select **Bring in facts from your ORCID record**. Data Director reads your public record from
   ORCID. Nothing is kept yet.
3. Tick what you want agents in this project to use: your name, other names, biography, keywords,
   country, web pages and other identifiers, and any of your affiliations, works and funding.
   Nothing is ticked until you tick it. **Tick all** ticks a whole group.
4. Select **Keep the ticked facts**.

Data Director reads your record from ORCID again when you keep it, and keeps ORCID's own words.
To change what is kept, edit your record at ORCID, then choose again. Only your 50 most recent
works are offered.

## Where it goes

What you keep is stored in the project, and anyone working on the project can see it. Every
agent run in the project is given it. The metadata librarian, for example, adds you as a creator
with your ORCID iD and current employer, your keywords as subjects and your grants as funding.

An agent that sends data to an AI model hosted outside your institution is given these facts
only if your institution's deployment allows it; otherwise they are left out.

Data Director never reads your email address from ORCID, even if you have made it public, and it
reads only your own record, only when you ask.

## Withdrawing

Select **Withdraw what you kept** under your record. Agents in the project are no longer given
it. Like everything in a project's record, what you kept stays in the project's history.
