# Asking a data steward

A data steward is a person at your institution who looks after research data. **Ask a data
steward** hands a matter from you to them: something an agent could not settle, a decision that
needs a person's judgement, or a question you would rather put to a colleague. The request is kept
in your project, and everything that happens to it is shown to you.

Stewards, not agents, decide what happens next. The steward's side of this is in the
[Data Steward's Guide](../steward-guide/escalations.md).

## When to ask

- **An agent referred or abstained.** A run that ends `referred` or `abstained` is a point where a
  person can help ([Understanding outcomes](outcomes.md)). **Ask a data steward** appears beside
  the run.
- **The conversation offers.** If the orchestrator sees that you need a person, it asks whether you
  would like to ask a data steward, with **Ask a data steward** and **Not now** as the answers.
- **You want to.** The **Data steward** section of the project panel, and every project page, has
  the same button. You do not need an agent to have failed first.

Nothing is sent when an offer appears. It is sent only when you press **Send to a data steward**.

!!! note "A step that needs approval"
    If your institution's policy stopped a step until a steward approves it, the step stays
    stopped whatever you choose. The form then offers **Leave it stopped** instead of **Not now**.

## Sending a request

1. Press **Ask a data steward**. The form opens with a note already written when you came from a
   run, which you can change.
2. Under **What would you like help with?**, say what you need, in your own words. A note is
   required.
3. If your institution has more than one steward team and none is chosen for the project, choose
   one under **Which data steward team?**.
4. Under **What will be shared**, read the list. It is the project's title, the recent conversation
   and the runs in it, and the matter itself when you came from a run. Untick anything you would
   rather not point the steward to.
5. Press **Send to a data steward**.

The form also says which team the request goes to, how soon that team usually answers, and what
opening the project means (next section).

If the form says **No data steward team is set up for this project**, nothing can be sent from
there. Ask whoever runs Data Director for you who looks after research data at your institution.

## What a steward can see

Unticking a line narrows what your note points the steward to. It does not hide the project.
**While your request is open, the team can open the whole project** to help you, as the same view
you have, and can write to you, propose edits to documents, and run agents in the project for you.
They cannot decide your project facts, approve or reject a draft, approve a deposit or change who is
a member.

You always see what they did:

- Each time a steward opens the project is recorded, once a day for each steward, and shown under
  **What data stewards did here** in the project panel.
- A steward's message in the conversation is marked **Data steward**, with their name.
- A document edit by a steward is a proposed version marked as theirs, for you to approve or
  reject.

See [What is recorded](records.md) and [Working with others](members.md).

## Following your request

Your request, with one status line, shows in the **Data steward** section of the project panel.
The line reads like "Sent to *Research Data Support* on 12 Nov 2026 · Taken by *Ada* · Waiting for
you to reply".

| State | What it means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| Waiting for a data steward to take it | Sent to the team; nobody has taken it yet. | **Withdraw** it. |
| A data steward is looking at it | A steward took it. | **Withdraw** it. |
| Waiting for you to reply | The steward asked you something. | **Reply**, or **Withdraw** it. |
| Resolved | The steward closed it, with a note. | **Re-open** it, with a reason, if you disagree. |
| Handed back to you | The steward judged the matter yours to take on, with a note. | **Re-open** it, with a reason. |
| Withdrawn | You withdrew it. | Ask again if you need to. |

**Reply** and **Re-open** need a note. **Withdraw** does not. Re-opening keeps the request's history.

A steward may pass your request to a colleague or to another team. The panel then says so, naming
the colleague or team. If nobody takes a request within the team's expected response time, it is
sent to the team again and you are told.

!!! note "Pauses"
    A steward can pause a project on their team's behalf. The panel then says why. While it lasts
    only advisers run, even if you withdraw your request, until a steward on that team lifts the
    pause.

## Being told

**Updates** in the banner tells you when a steward asks you something, writes to you, resolves a
request or hands it back, and when a request nobody had taken is sent to the team again. Data
Director keeps no email address for you, so it tells you here rather than by email. Choose which of
these it shows on the Settings page, under **Notifications**.

## With the keyboard

Every control above can be reached with Tab and pressed with Enter or Space. The checkboxes under
**What will be shared** and the team choice in the form are ordinary form controls. What happens to a request (sent, replied, withdrawn, re-opened) is
announced to screen readers.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This has already been sent to a data steward."** The run already has a request. Its status is
  shown in place of the button.
- **"This request has moved on since you last saw it."** A steward acted while you were writing.
  The panel has been read again: look at the new state and try again.
- **A send fails for another reason.** Your note is kept in the form. Try again, or ask whoever runs
  Data Director for you.
