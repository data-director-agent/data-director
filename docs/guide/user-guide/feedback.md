# Giving feedback

Data Director is built with the people who manage research data. Judgement is as useful to the
project as code: whether advice suits a discipline, whether a decision suits an institution,
whether a requirement of the Blueprint is met. Each kind of feedback has a form on GitHub. You
need a GitHub account, and what you write is public.

Do not include personal data, unpublished research or anything confidential.

| You want to | Form |
|---|---|
| Say that an agent's advice was wrong, incomplete or unhelpful, and what good advice would have been | [Feedback on advice](https://github.com/data-director-agent/data-director/issues/new?template=advice-feedback.yml) |
| Report a review of whether a requirement is met, such as accessibility or the quality of standards advice | [Conformance review](https://github.com/data-director-agent/data-director/issues/new?template=conformance-review.yml) |
| Question or object to a decision about how Data Director is built | [Decision record feedback](https://github.com/data-director-agent/data-director/issues/new?template=adr-feedback.yml) |
| Point out where Data Director reads the Blueprint differently from you, or misses a requirement | [Blueprint reading](https://github.com/data-director-agent/data-director/issues/new?template=blueprint-reading.yml) |
| Report something that does not work, or ask for something new | [Bug](https://github.com/data-director-agent/data-director/issues/new?template=bug.yml) or [Feature](https://github.com/data-director-agent/data-director/issues/new?template=feature.yml), or **Report a problem** in the app |

All the forms are listed on [one page](https://github.com/data-director-agent/data-director/issues/new/choose).

## Reviews

A conformance review records what one person examined and found, at one version. A maintainer
adds it to the review record on your instruction, and it appears in the
[conformance report](../../../CONFORMANCE.md) beside the results of the automated tests.
[How conformance is assessed](../../conformance.md) explains what a review can and cannot show.
A finding that a requirement is not met is as useful as one that it is.

## Decisions and the Blueprint

The project's [decision records](../../adr/README.md) say how Data Director is built and why.
You do not need to propose an alternative design to object to one: saying who would be affected,
and how, is enough.

The [Blueprint](../../blueprint/README.md) itself is maintained by its RDA working group. The
Blueprint reading form is for how this project interprets it; to change the Blueprint's text,
contact the working group.
