# Test data from re3data

`re3data-export-fixture.zip`, `r3d100010230.xml` and `r3d100012124.xml` are records cut,
unchanged, from re3data's export of November 2026
(<https://www.re3data.org/export/re3data-repositories-2026-11.zip>), in metadata schema 4.0
(<https://doi.org/10.48440/re3.014>).

re3data.org - Registry of Research Data Repositories. <https://doi.org/10.17616/R3D>. Its
records are published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); we
credit re3data nonetheless.
