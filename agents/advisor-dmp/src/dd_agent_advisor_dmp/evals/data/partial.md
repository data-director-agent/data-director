# Data Management Plan: Community Health Conversations

Synthetic plan written for the advisor.dmp evaluation. The project, people and data are invented.

## Data to be collected

We will run an online survey of around 400 residents and conduct 30 semi-structured interviews,
which will be audio recorded and transcribed. Transcripts will be anonymised before analysis.

## Ethics and consent

Participants will consent to the use of their anonymised data in research and to its sharing
with other researchers under appropriate safeguards.

## Sharing and preservation

Survey responses will be shared openly in ORDA, the University of Sheffield's repository, under
CC BY 4.0 once the main findings are published.

The anonymised interview transcripts will be deposited in the UK Data Service under safeguarded
access.

The analysis scripts will be made available on request.

The audio recordings will not be shared and will be destroyed once the transcripts have been
checked.

## Long-term preservation

The repositories named above will preserve the shared data for at least ten years.
